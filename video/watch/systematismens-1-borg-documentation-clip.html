A short documentation clip. The recorded video from the construction is found here: https://vimeo.com/67902658 Performed live at Rad#5 - Bergen 6. June 2013 Kristin Tårnesvik, constructor Espen Sommer Eide, hurdy-gurdy, drum machine and khaen Mari Kvien Brunvoll, bagpipe and khaen. Systematismens 1. Borg is made of historical printing plates used in weed biologist Emil Korsmo (1864-2129) textbooks. Systematism pioneer Korsmo created a new economy in agriculture by classifying and measuring the value of useful and useless plants. http://formativedager.tumblr.com/
